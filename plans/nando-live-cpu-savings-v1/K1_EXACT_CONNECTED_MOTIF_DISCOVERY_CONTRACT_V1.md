# K1 Exact Connected Motif Discovery Contract V1

Status: preregistered architecture contract. No Law #2 claim.

Date: 2026-08-10.

## Decision

K1 discovery will stop treating the complete request-specific role graph as the
only unit of natural repetition. A new versioned lane may cohort bounded exact
connected pre-action motifs across different ambient graphs while preserving
the complete topology and every historical root unchanged.

```text
immutable complete topology
-> bounded connected induced motifs, pre-action only
-> operator-blind frozen motif domain
-> Raw Phase program generation after freeze
-> exact consumed-role / motif-embedding binding
-> exact replay across support
-> independent future on a new lineage
-> transfer-ready terminal, authority false
-> canonical BundleV4 candidate -> external admission
-> typed CPU before upstream -> independent verifier
-> durable CPU completion + exact economics receipt
-> exact-memory cleanup verifier -> LawCertificate
```

The motif is an evidence domain, not a program hint. `OperatorIdentificationMachineV1`
remains the only identifier.

## Finding That Requires The Change

The live join and settlement routes are healthy. The current scheduler groups
natural rows by the complete topology, so a repeatable local law embedded in
different request-specific context is divided into mostly singleton cohorts.

The exact live frontier before this change was:

```text

```

A read-only census over the same natural archives found:

This proves that the archive contains repeated local structure. It does not
prove that any motif causes its consequence, that a unique law exists, or that
Law #2 has been found.

## Version Boundary

Historical Evidence V1-V3, Candidate V1-V3, Freeze V1-V5, generator bases, and
ledgers retain their original decoding, roots, and replay behavior. They are
never reinterpreted as motif evidence.

The new route uses:

```text
K1NaturalEvidenceRowV4
K1NaturalCohortCandidateV4
K1NaturalCandidateFreezeV6
RawPhaseHypothesisGeneratorV4

```

Duplicate identity is scoped to:

```text
candidate structural identity
+ epistemic-known protocol-mode set root
+ DiscoveryBasis root
```

The V4 basis may reopen an old complete-topology terminal only through the
existing versioned duplicate rule. It never modifies that terminal.

## Exact Motif Semantics

A motif is a connected induced subgraph of one complete pre-action topology.
Its canonical root commits:

```text
motif schema
canonical source-neutral role classes
canonical induced relation edges
role count
relation count
enumeration configuration root
```

V1 bounds each motif to at most four roles. Enumeration inspects neither role
values nor terminal outcomes. Isomorphic role-ID permutations share a motif
root; non-isomorphic graphs never merge.

For every complete topology row, the descriptor retains all exact embeddings
of each emitted motif root, not merely the lexicographically smallest one. One
embedding commits the sorted ambient `local_role_id` set and the immutable
complete topology root. Embeddings are canonical, deduplicated, and strictly
ordered.

Disconnected projections, post-action roles, inferred values, source names,
operation names, package names, and physical program names are forbidden from
motif construction.

## Evidence V4

Every V4 evidence row binds:

```text
historical evidence root and capture generation
complete topology root and complete joined-row provenance
motif root
all exact embeddings for that motif in the complete topology
embedding-manifest root
consequence type
semantic novelty signature
lineage and capture/contract sequences
settled, verified, safety, and fixture provenance
input-token denominator
```

The complete topology remains available for audit and future applicability. A
motif row cannot replace or weaken its ambient evidence.

Support retention is bounded to 64 distinct complete rows per motif candidate.
Rows are retained deterministically by capture sequence and row root. Every
additional valid row receives `MOTIF_SUPPORT_OVERFLOW` with a count and rolling
manifest root; it is not silently discarded or used to alter frozen support.

Every scanned row receives exactly one durable motif-lane disposition:

```text
MOTIF_RETAINED
MOTIF_SUPPORT_OVERFLOW
CENSORED_EMPTY_OR_INCOMPLETE_TOPOLOGY
CENSORED_MOTIF_ENUMERATION_BUDGET
CENSORED_INVALID_EMBEDDING
FIXTURE_OR_CONTROLLED_EXCLUDED
SAFETY_VETO
```

The per-row enumeration bound remains explicit. A budget breach censors that
row with its complete topology root; it does not silently truncate motif output.

## Candidate V4 And Freeze V6

The operator-blind V4 candidate identity commits:

```text
capture generation
motif structural root
consequence type
semantic novelty signature
evidence manifest root
embedding-manifest root
readiness receipt
support and overflow dispositions
```

Pre-freeze ranking retains the existing order and hard vetoes. It may use K1
deficit, readiness, bounded discovery cost, verified token opportunity, and a
stable hash. It may not use a generated program, physical operator family, or
active package identity.

Freeze V6 additionally binds:

```text
complete catalog root and motif catalog root
motif schema and enumeration configuration root
motif support/embedding manifests
Evidence V4, Candidate V4, Generator V4, and DiscoveryBasis V4 roots
epistemic registry and known-mode CAS roots
K1 deficit and fixture-exclusion roots
budget, support watermark, contract watermark, future_min_sequence
```

The authority independently reconstructs all roots before append. Any mismatch
is `STALE_BEFORE_FREEZE` or a fail-closed authority error.

## Program-To-Motif Binding

After freeze, Raw Phase may generate only from the frozen support. Every typed
hypothesis must expose one canonical consumed-role-ID set for every support row,
including implicit selectors and renderer selectors.

A hypothesis is admissible on a row only when:

```text
consumed role IDs are non-empty
AND every consumed role is pre-action grounded
AND one exact frozen motif embedding contains the entire consumed-role set
AND every selector/value binding resolves inside that same embedding
```

Collection and non-collection programs use the same generic extractor and the
same containment rule. A role outside the embedding is a hard exclusion, not a
replay miss. A program cannot combine roles from two different embeddings.

This binding is checked during hypothesis construction, exact support replay,
selected-receipt sealing, actor reconstruction, future prediction, and
certification authority replay. Caller-provided role sets have no authority.

## Identification And Future

Complete ambient graphs may differ across support rows. Cohorting across them is
allowed only for programs whose consumed roles fit an exact embedding of the
same frozen motif root on every replayed row.

The existing exact version space and semantic quotient remain authoritative:

```text
0 semantic classes  -> ABSTAIN / representation gap
1 semantic class    -> frozen unique law, still no authority
>1 classes          -> distinguishing probe
```

Future applicability requires a new-lineage complete pre-action topology after
`future_min_sequence`, an exact occurrence of the frozen motif, a complete
pre-action binding inside one embedding, and a durable prediction before the
terminal outcome. Missing, ambiguous, or outside-embedding data censors the
case and never becomes anti-evidence.

Only independent PASS may produce a transfer-ready terminal and canonical
BundleV4 candidate for external admission. LawCertificate authority remains
false until verified ordinary CPU-before-upstream execution, durable completion
and exact economics receipts, and the exact-memory cleanup verifier all pass.

## Acceptance Evidence

Implementation is incomplete until current-state evidence proves all of:

1. Historical V1-V5 bytes and roots survive decoding and replay unchanged.
2. Isomorphic local motifs share a root while non-isomorphic motifs do not.
3. Every exact embedding is retained, canonical, and ambient-topology bound.
4. Enumeration budget and support overflow have explicit durable dispositions.
5. Candidate ranking remains operator-blind and fixture/safety fail-closed.
6. A generated program using any outside role is rejected for collection,
   scalar, record, boolean, and rendered-sequence consequences.
7. Exact replay may cross ambient graphs only through one common frozen motif.
8. Future prediction requires a post-watermark exact embedding and precommit.
9. Authority reconstructs the consumed-role binding and all V4/V6 CAS roots.
10. Restart preserves active-generation, ledger, support, embedding, and
    prediction roots byte-for-byte.
11. Live autonomous scheduling produces a V6 generation or gives every ready
    motif candidate an exact terminal veto.
12. Law #2 is claimed only after genuine independent future, ordinary verified
    CPU execution, exact economics, cleanup, LawCertificate, and zero false
    accepts.
