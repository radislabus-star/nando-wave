## 3. Strongest Remaining Alternative Explanation

Even after PASS, a competent engineer could write an explicit source-neutral
heuristic over the same relation atoms. This experiment does not compare every
possible heuristic and therefore cannot prove that learning is the only route.
It proves the narrower causal fact that parameters learned from train decisions
are sufficient and necessary for this frozen policy under the frozen budget.

The correct interpretation is:

```text
learned compact ranking representation beats complete enumeration in search
denominator while retaining exact confirmatory execution
```

The forbidden interpretation is:

```text
Nanda now understands arbitrary meaning
```
