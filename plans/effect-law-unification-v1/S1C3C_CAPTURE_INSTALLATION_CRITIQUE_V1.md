## Accepted Architecture

```text
S1C-3C paper identity
-> pure local schema preflight
-> new attempt namespace
-> frozen S1C-3B mechanism as a pinned dependency
-> independent mechanism verification
-> S1C-3C authority envelope
-> resource VETO or transactional deployment
-> final independent verification
-> factual operational projection
```

The wrapper may reuse mechanism code but may not change its candidate,
thresholds, measurement order, denominator, production binary/config target,
or rollback behavior. The implementation gate must detect any such drift by
hash and invariant tests.
