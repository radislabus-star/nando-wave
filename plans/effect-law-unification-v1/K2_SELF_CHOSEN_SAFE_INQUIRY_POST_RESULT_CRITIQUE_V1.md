## 4. Process Independence

executable hashes. Selector and verifier share canonical data types and hashes,

functions. It separately implements eligibility, effect application,
prediction grouping, ranking, baseline reconstruction, and elimination.

The driver remains a material trusted boundary: after independent selection
verification it resolves the selected opaque action through the private true
model and gives that one effect to the worker. The observer receives no such
mapping. This is adequate for the frozen question, but not evidence that Nanda
induced the private model during this experiment.
